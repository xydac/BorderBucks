# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

BorderBucks welcomes participants of every background and experience level.
Treat people with respect, give specific and constructive feedback, and discuss
tax interpretations and software behavior on their evidence rather than attacking
the person raising them. Be clear about uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexual attention, personal attacks,
deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information
are not acceptable. Do not pressure anyone to share financial statements or
identifying information. Do not present project participation as an endorsement
of professional qualifications or the correctness of a tax return.

These standards apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
project-managed spaces, and when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting And Enforcement

For immediate public moderation, use GitHub's reporting tools on the offending
content. Do not repeat private information or publish a sensitive incident report
in an issue. No dedicated confidential conduct contact is currently published;
use GitHub's platform reporting/support channels for sensitive incidents rather
than assuming that a public maintainer mention is private. Security vulnerabilities
use the separate route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove content, request a correction, warn participants,
restrict participation temporarily, or ban participants for serious or repeated
violations. Responses should consider impact, severity, and repetition. Maintainers
are expected to follow the same standards and avoid handling incidents in which
they have a conflict of interest. Confidentiality cannot be guaranteed on public
project channels.
